📷Camera Access Needed

Allow camera permission in your browser to scan photos in Augmented Reality.

  1. iPhone (Safari): Tap aA in address bar → Website Settings → Camera: Allow
  2. Android (Chrome): Tap the Lock icon in address bar → Permissions → Camera: Allow
